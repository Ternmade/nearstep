Clear only this browser’s saved count? This cannot change any records in the app.

Your chosen start is stored only in this browser, when storage is available. It is not sent to Ternmade, and it does not sync with the app. Clearing browser data can remove it.

What does “no contact” mean here?

You choose the boundary. A count does not decide whether a conversation is necessary, and it does not measure your recovery. Shared responsibilities, work and safety still matter.

If you want a fresh count here, clear it and start again. In the Nearstep app, logging that you reached out or replied restarts the current count while keeping your history. Necessary contact does not restart the app’s count.
