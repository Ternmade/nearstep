Start with what helps now

  • A free counter that keeps ticking through the first minutes, hours and days.
  • A ten-minute pause and private words you can leave unsent.
  • A free Home Screen widget for a glance at your count.
  • Your history and skies stay when the current count restarts. Necessary contact does not restart it.

Nearstep supports Android 7 and later. The app also runs on iPhone and iPad, with the same core features. Your writing stays encrypted on each device; there is no cloud sync between them.

Make a plan before the hard moment

Optional Nearstep Plus helps you decide what to do when the urge returns and prepare for hard dates or encounters. Your plan appears inside your next pause. One purchase, no subscription and no trial; the store shows your local price before you buy.

The counter, pause, unsent writing and widget stay free. Saved personal results remain available to read and edit.
